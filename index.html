Hi, I'm Zahy.

I'm drawn to honest, unposed moments: the second before a laugh, the way evening light falls across a street, the calm of the sea before a storm.

Whether it's a portrait session, a wedding or a personal travel series, I work slowly and with intention, so every frame feels like it belongs to the person in it.
